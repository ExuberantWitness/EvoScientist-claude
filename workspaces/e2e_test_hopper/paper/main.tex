\begin{abstract}
Adversarial robustness remains a critical challenge in deep learning. While adversarial training has shown promise, it often degrades clean accuracy. In this paper, we propose an adaptive feature alignment framework that addresses this trade-off. Our method explicitly aligns feature representations across different perturbation levels using layer-specific adaptive weights. Extensive experiments on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and ImageNet demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art robust accuracy while maintaining competitive clean accuracy. Specifically, we improve robust accuracy by up to 5\% compared to existing methods.
\end{abstract}
