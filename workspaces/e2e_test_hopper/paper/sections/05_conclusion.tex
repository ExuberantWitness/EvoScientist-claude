\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we proposed an adaptive feature alignment framework for adversarial robustness. Our method addresses the robustness-accuracy trade-off by explicitly aligning feature representations across different perturbation levels, with layer-specific adaptive weights. Experimental results on multiple benchmark datasets demonstrate that our approach achieves state-of-the-art robust accuracy while maintaining competitive clean accuracy.

Future work includes extending our framework to other architectures and exploring the theoretical connections between feature alignment and certified robustness. We also plan to investigate the application of our method to other domains such as natural language processing and reinforcement learning.
