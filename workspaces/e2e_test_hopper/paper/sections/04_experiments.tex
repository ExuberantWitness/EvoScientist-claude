\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
We evaluate our method on three benchmark datasets: CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100 \cite{krizhevsky2009learning}, and ImageNet \cite{deng2009imagenet}. We use ResNet-50 \cite{he2016deep} as the backbone architecture. For adversarial training, we use PGD-10 with $\epsilon = 8/255$ and step size $2/255$. The regularization coefficient $\lambda$ is set to 0.1. We train for 100 epochs using SGD with momentum 0.9, weight decay 5e-4, and initial learning rate 0.1 with cosine annealing.
